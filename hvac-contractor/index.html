Start with the problem you’re noticing

An HVAC contractor can inspect your cooling system, identify what may be causing trouble, and explain possible repair options. If your AC isn’t keeping up, note what happens and when. Does the issue start as soon as you turn the system on, or does it appear later? Does it happen every time? These details give the technician a clearer starting point.

Before you call, write down the system’s symptoms and any changes you’ve noticed. You don’t need to diagnose the cause yourself. A clear description is more useful than guessing which part has failed.

What to have ready for the visit

A little preparation can help the appointment go smoothly. Before the technician arrives, consider this checklist:

  • Make a note of symptoms, when they began, and whether they come and go.
  • Share any recent changes you’ve noticed in how the system runs.
  • Make sure the technician can reach the AC equipment and thermostat.
  • Think about the questions you want answered, including what the inspection found and what repair choices are available.

Avoid taking equipment apart or working on electrical components to try to find the fault. A reset that only helps briefly is also not a diagnosis. Leave inspection and repair work to a trained professional, and tell us what you already tried.
