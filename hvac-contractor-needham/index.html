Get ready before the technician arrives

You don’t need special tools or technical knowledge to prepare. A few observations can make it easier to describe what’s going on:

  • Note when the issue first appeared and whether it has changed.
  • Describe what happens when you use the AC, including anything unusual you’ve noticed.
  • Make a list of questions about the inspection, repair options, and timing.
  • Ensure the thermostat and equipment are accessible for the visit.

One mistake to avoid is repeatedly trying to fix a problem without knowing its cause. Don’t take apart the system or attempt electrical work. If you’ve already tried something, let the technician know so that information can be considered during the assessment.
