What to expect and how to prepare

When you arrange a maintenance visit, describe the equipment and the kind of service you’re looking for. A technician can review the system and discuss its condition with you. The exact needs can vary, so ask what the visit includes and whether there are concerns that call for a separate repair.

Before the appointment, make a short note of:
- Any changes in heating or cooling performance.
- Sounds, smells, or airflow concerns you have noticed.
- Areas of the home that feel less comfortable than usual.
- Questions about the equipment or work being recommended.
- Any known service or repair history you can share.

Please don’t try to take apart equipment or reach into areas that aren’t meant for homeowner access. If the system has stopped, is behaving strangely, or you’re unsure what’s safe to do, contact a professional instead.
